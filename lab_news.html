Lab News

Oct. 2026 — We are excited to announce that Ang Li will join our lab as a postdoc in Nov. 2026. Ang recently completed his Ph.D. in Systems, Synthetic, and Quantitative Biology at Harvard University, working with Dr. Michael Springer.

Sep. 2026 — We are delighted to announce that Lin will join our lab as a postdoc in Jan. 2027, after completing her Ph.D. at Virginia Tech, where she studies circadian rhythms. We look forward to welcoming Lin to the lab.

Jul. 2026 — Congratulations to Peter on winning a poster competition, only 4 awards selected from 64 international entries, at the FASEB Genetic Recombination and Genome Rearrangements Conference in Scottsdale, AZ.

Jun. 2026 — Our lab received a highly competitive, four-year Department of Defense (DoD) CDMRP Idea Development Award for the project “Defining an Error-free DNA Repair Signature as a Predictive Marker for Lethal Prostate Cancer.” This award supports our efforts to develop new approaches for predicting DNA repair vulnerabilities in prostate cancer.

Mar. 2026 — Peter and Shiyang presented their research at the DNA Damage, Mutation and Cancer GRC. Congratulations to Peter on winning second place in the highly competitive poster competition, selected from 93 international entries.

Feb. 2026 — A major milestone for our lab: Peter, Trevor, and Yi published their study, “High-throughput mapping of spontaneous mitotic crossover and genome instability events with sci-L3-Strand-seq,” in Nucleic Acids Research. This work establishes a scalable approach and a comprehensive computational suite for studying spontaneous mitotic crossover and genome instability at single-cell resolution.
