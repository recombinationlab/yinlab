Joining the Lab

We have openings for students, postdocs and technicians. Please contact Yi Yin, if interested.

Postdoctoral Fellows
Prospective postdocs should submit a CV, a description of their past and future research interests, and the names and contact information for at least two references.

Graduate Students
The lab accepts rotation students from Spring 2020. Prospective students should submit a CV, a description of their past and future research interests and all graduate coursework. More details to come.

Technicians
Please submit a one-page summary of research experience, lab skills and contact information for at least two references.
